# Privacy And Disclaimer Policy Notes

## Privacy Defaults

- No account required
- Local data storage by default
- Hidden notification previews supported
- PIN lock supported

## Sensitive Surface Rules

- Third-party ad surfaces are allowed only through AdMob and Google UMP consent flows
- Cycle dates, symptoms, mood, medication, notes, profile settings, and exports must not be passed to ad SDKs
- AdMob and UMP may process ad consent, device, network, and advertising-related data under Google's policies
- Avoid showing health details in lock-screen notifications
- Minimize analytics and keep them removable behind interfaces

## Disclaimers

- CycleCare provides estimates and trend summaries for wellness tracking
- It is not intended for diagnosis, contraception decisions, or emergency guidance
