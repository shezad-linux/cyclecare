📋 1. Overview

CycleCare is a local-first menstrual cycle and wellness tracking application for Android. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data the app handles, what stays on your device, and how ad privacy choices work.

Effective Date: 19 October 2026 (previous version: 23 March 2026)

📝 2. Information We Collect

CycleCare does not collect, transmit, or store your cycle and wellness information on remote servers. All health data you enter — including cycle dates, symptoms, mood, medication, and notes — is stored exclusively on your device.

  • No account or sign-up required — the app works immediately after install.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting product — we do not embed Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or any similar third-party analytics product.
  • Ads and consent — we do embed Google AdMob and the Google User Messaging Platform. AdMob can read your device's advertising identifier and collect device, network, and usage data to serve and measure ads, and Google's ad services may use it to personalise ads to you based on activity across other apps and websites. See Data Sharing.

💾 3. How Your Data Is Stored

All health and cycle data is persisted in a local database on your device. Data is never uploaded to the cloud unless you explicitly choose to export it.

  • Data remains under your control. It is not accessible to us, because we do not operate a server that receives it.
  • Cloud backup is switched off, so your data is not copied to Google's cloud backup service.
  • Device-to-device transfer is enabled on Android 12 and higher, so when you move to a new phone your cycle data can be copied to it directly. If that happens, uninstalling the app from your old phone does not delete the copy on the new one.
  • On Android 8.0 to 11, both cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled, so uninstalling removes all app data from the device.
  • Optional data export produces files that are saved to your device's local storage, wherever you choose.

🔗 4. Data Sharing

CycleCare does not share, sell, or transfer your cycle history, symptoms, notes, profile details, or exported reports to third parties. Google AdMob and Google UMP are used for ads and ad privacy choices, and they may process ad consent, device, network, and advertising-related data under Google's policies.

🔑 5. App Permissions

CycleCare requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:

  • Notifications — Used to send optional cycle and wellness reminders that you configure. Notification content is designed to be discreet; no sensitive health details are shown on the lock screen.
  • Boot Completed — Used to reschedule your reminders after a device restart so you never miss a notification you set up.
  • Exact alarms (special access) — Used so that reminders you have configured arrive at the time you chose rather than at a later, batched time. This is a special "app access" permission, not a runtime prompt, and Android may revoke it at any time. CycleCare degrades to inexact delivery if it is revoked, and you can grant or withdraw it from Android Settings.
CycleCare requests Internet access for ads and ad privacy consent, and network state to detect connectivity. It does not request Camera, Location, Contacts, Microphone, or other sensitive permissions.

🛡️ 6. Security

We take the following measures to protect your data:

  • PIN Lock — An optional in-app PIN lock prevents unauthorised access to the app.
  • Hidden Notification Previews — Sensitive information is hidden from lock-screen notification previews.
  • Local Health Data — Cycle history, symptoms, notes, profile details, and exports are not sent to the ad SDK.

👶 7. Children's Privacy

CycleCare is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Cycle and wellness data is stored locally and is not transmitted to CycleCare servers.

⚕️ 8. Health Disclaimer

CycleCare provides estimates and trend summaries for personal wellness tracking only. The app is not intended to be used for:

  • Medical diagnosis or treatment decisions
  • Contraception planning or fertility advice
  • Emergency health guidance
Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice. CycleCare is a wellness tool, not a medical device.
